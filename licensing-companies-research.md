## PART 2 — What "licensing company" actually means (so we stay aligned)

The unifying thread across Snafu / Kurate / Broke: **a temporary license, not a permanent sale.**
The company gets a term license to the recordings + provides capital / marketing / distribution; the
artist **keeps ownership** and **shares revenue.** Mostafa's mandate = *licensing AND buyouts*. The
buyer rolodex = buyouts. **This document = the licensing side.**

But "licensing" splits into **5 distinct sub-models**, and each has a *different* target list. The 3
seed companies fall into A / B / C:

| # | Sub-model | What the company does | Seed example |
|---|-----------|----------------------|--------------|
| **A** | **License / services-deal labels** (keep-your-masters labels) | License + distribute + market; artist keeps ownership | Broke, Kurate, AWAL, UnitedMasters |
| **B** | **Music financing / royalty advances** (keep masters) | Cash advance vs. future royalties; revenue-share | **Snafu**, beatBread, Sound Royalties |
| **C** | **Digital-first / YouTube-TikTok marketing labels** | Same as A but social-media-native growth engine | **Broke**, Homemade Projects, Heroic |
| **D** | **Creator / royalty-free license labels** | License music *OUT to content creators* (different counterparty) | NCS, Monstercat, Proximity |
| **E** | **Sync licensing companies** | License for TV / film / ads / games placements | Position Music, Songtradr, Musicbed |

**Scope decision needed (see Part 5):** Snafu=B, Broke=A+C, Kurate=A. So Mostafa's "in the likes of"
points at **A + B + C.** D and E are real "licensing" but a different counterparty/relationship — include only if Mostafa wants them.

## PART 3 — The discovery system (how to find EVERY company, repeatably)

### Step 1 — The one filter test
> *"Will this company do a NON-BUYOUT deal with an indie artist — license / distribute / finance
> their music, let them keep ownership, and share revenue?"*
If yes → it belongs. If they only buy outright → that's the buyout rolodex. If they only sign new
unknown talent (traditional label) → skip.

### Step 2 — Seed-and-expand (the core engine)
For every known company, harvest its neighbors:
1. **LinkedIn company page → "Pages people also viewed"** (right rail). This surfaced Duetti↔SongVest↔RoyFi in your earlier work — it's the fastest expander.
2. **Crunchbase "Similar companies"** and **Pitchbook competitor sets.**
3. **Signing / deal announcements** → note JV partners and "also bid" companies.
Repeat on each new name. The list snowballs to the full universe in ~3–4 passes.

### Step 3 — Master source list (pull names systematically)
- **Merlin Network member directory** (merlinnetwork.org) — the global indie distribution/label universe in one list. *Highest-yield single source.*
- **A2IM member directory** (a2im.org) — American indie labels/distributors.
- **WIN — Worldwide Independent Network** (winformusic.org) — global indie bodies.
- **MusicBusinessWorldwide** — search tags: "distribution deal", "licensing deal", "artist services", "royalty advance".
- **Billboard Pro** — Indie Power Players + directories.
- **Rostr.cc** — filter company type = *Distributor* / *Label Services* (no acquisition filter, but works for distro/licensing).
- **Crunchbase / Pitchbook** — category "Music" + keywords "artist services" / "music financing" / "distribution".
- **Variety VIP, Music Ally, Digital Music News, The Bag (Substack)** — deal coverage.
- **UK Companies House / OpenCorporates** — verify UK firms (e.g., Kurate) and pull director names.

### Step 4 — Search-string playbook (run on Google + MBW)
- `"[genre] licensing deal" indie artist keep masters`
- `artist services deal "retain ownership"`
- `distribution deal advance indie label 2026`
- `royalty advance streaming "keep your masters"`
- `label services company [country/region]`
- `[company name] competitors OR alternatives OR "similar companies"`
- `"signed a" "distribution deal" OR "licensing deal" site:musicbusinessworldwide.com`

### Step 5 — Log + verify (one row per company)
Columns: Company · **Verified domain (check exact TLD)** · LinkedIn · Sub-model (A/B/C/D/E) ·
Deal type · Min artist size · Genre/specialty · Broker-friendly? · Key contacts · Last verified.
(Same schema as your buyer rolodex so the two files merge cleanly.)
